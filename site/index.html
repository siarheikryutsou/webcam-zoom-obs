Smooth webcam zoom in OBS: hold the mouse and zoom to that point

Webcam Zoom is a small panel inside OBS Studio. You see your camera picture in it, hold the left mouse button on a face, a hand or an object, and the webcam in your stream or recording smoothly zooms right there. Release it and the zoom stays. Right button zooms out.

⬇ Download ZIP Install in 5 minutes GitHub

What is it and who is it for?

If you stream, record tutorials, product reviews, cooking or crafts, sometimes you want the viewer to look closer: at an object in your hands, at your face, at a detail on the table. Usually that means a second camera or fiddling with crop settings during the stream. Webcam Zoom turns it into one mouse gesture.

🎯

Zoom to a point

Hold the left button where you want to look. The longer you hold, the closer it gets.

🌊

Smooth like a camera operator

All movements are animated. Speed, maximum zoom and smoothness are adjustable sliders.

🎬

Every scene at once

Fullscreen camera, small camera in a corner: the zoom is applied to all of them in sync.

🧩

No plugins

Just a web page in an OBS dock. It uses obs-websocket, which is already built into OBS.

How to install (step by step)

You need OBS Studio 28 or newer (Help → About shows the version). Nothing else.

  1. Download and unpack

    Click Download ZIP. Unpack it to a folder you won't delete, for example C:\obs-tools\webcam-zoom-obs. Inside there must be a file index.html.

  2. Turn on the WebSocket server in OBS

    In OBS: Tools → WebSocket Server Settings. Tick Enable WebSocket server. Click Show Connect Info and copy the password. The port is usually 4455. Click OK.

  3. Add the dock

    In OBS: Docks → Custom Browser Docks…. In the empty row type the name Webcam Zoom and the URL to the file, with forward slashes:

    file:///C:/obs-tools/webcam-zoom-obs/index.html

    Click Apply. A new panel appears. Drag it wherever you like.

    On macOS / Linux the URL looks like file:///Users/you/obs-tools/webcam-zoom-obs/index.html.

  4. Connect

    In the panel, the address is already ws://127.0.0.1:4455. Paste the password and click Connect («Подключить»). The status turns green: online.

    The panel interface is currently in Russian. On this page button names are given with their Russian labels in brackets.

  5. Pick your camera

    Choose any scene and the item with your webcam (it is usually selected automatically). The camera picture appears in the panel. Done. Next time the panel connects by itself.

How to use

ActionWhat happens
Hold Left mouse button on the pictureZooms in smoothly towards the cursor. Move the mouse while holding and the zoom follows.
Release the buttonThe zoom stays where it is.
Hold Right mouse buttonZooms out smoothly, also following the cursor.
Drag the ✥ handle in the corner of the blue frameMoves the visible area without changing the zoom.
Mouse wheelZoom in or out step by step.
Double-clickSmoothly back to the full picture.

The panel always shows the whole camera picture. The blue frame is what your viewers see right now.

Settings

Click the panel header to open settings: Speed («Скорость», how fast the zoom grows while you hold), Max zoom («Макс. зум», up to ×10), Smoothness («Плавность», how softly the picture catches up), and Restore original view («Вернуть исходный вид»).

Several scenes

The same webcam can be fullscreen in one scene and small in a corner in another. The panel finds all of them (including in groups and nested scenes) and zooms them together. The small camera stays small, only the picture inside it gets closer. New scenes with the camera are picked up automatically.

When the panel takes over a camera item, it replaces that item's own crop and bounding box. Restore original view brings them back for every item.

How it works (for the curious)

OBS preview does not pass mouse clicks into sources, and a Browser Source only accepts the mouse through a separate “Interact” window. A custom browser dock is a normal interactive web page inside OBS, so Webcam Zoom lives there.

The dock talks to OBS through obs-websocket (built in since OBS 28). It never opens your camera by itself: OBS keeps it. The preview in the dock is a stream of screenshots of the camera source. Zoom is done by changing the crop of the camera scene item, while a bounding box keeps its size on the canvas. All the animation runs in the dock.

Everything stays on your computer. The dock connects only to 127.0.0.1, your own OBS.

FAQ and troubleshooting

The panel says “Failed to connect” («Не удалось подключиться»)

Check that the WebSocket server is enabled in Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, that the port is 4455 and the password is exactly the one shown in Show Connect Info.

The panel is empty / white

The file path in the dock URL is wrong. It must start with file:///, use forward slashes and point to index.html inside the unpacked folder.

The preview is black or the zoom does nothing

Make sure the right item is selected in the settings and the camera is actually working in OBS. Press ⟳ to refresh the lists.

Zoom works in one scene but not in another

Press ⟳ in the settings. The panel will rescan all scenes and pick up every copy of the webcam.

How do I remove it?

Click Restore original view, then remove the dock in Docks → Custom Browser Docks and delete the folder.

Is it an OBS plugin? Is it safe?

It's not a plugin, it's a local HTML page. It does not send anything to the internet and does not open your webcam directly.
